- **Behavior:** An active campaign accepts a fresh PB-16-verified receipt before the policy claim deadline. Submission reserves exactly one grant from unallocated funds, consumes the receipt atomically, and fixes the payout recipient from the receipt's eligibility-bound leaf. Submission and finalization transfer no tokens. Anyone can finalize only at or after the submission-time challenge cutoff. Only the fixed recipient can withdraw an awarded grant. The state and accounting update roll back if token transfer fails or either balance delta differs from the grant. Claims for different deployment keys progress independently.
- **Replay and invalid-input handling:** The campaign rejects an already claimed deployment, while PB-16 enforces campaign/receipt replay protection and receipt purpose, result, mode, signature, freshness, policy and eligibility checks. A failed validation reverts the reservation and receipt consumption together.
