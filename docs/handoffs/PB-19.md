# PB-19 handoff — bounded challenge, review and recovery

- **Task:** PB-19 only. PB-20 cross-chain message transport remains separate.
- **Files:** `contracts/pb17/IRemediationCampaign.sol`, `contracts/pb17/RemediationCampaign.sol`, and `contracts/test/pb19/`.
- **Behavior:** The supplier or policy-configured challenger may dispute a Pending claim strictly before its submission-time challenge cutoff. The cutoff never moves. The configured independent reviewer may award or reject before a fixed review deadline. Award moves exactly one grant from reserved to awarded; rejection moves exactly one grant from reserved back to unallocated. Anyone may expire a disputed claim at or after the review deadline; timeout rejects and releases the reservation. `reviewDeadlineOf` exposes the fixed review deadline, including after resolution for history.
- **Recovery:** At or after claim deadline D, the supplier may pull only current unallocated funds. Pending, Disputed, and Awarded funds remain reserved/protected. Refund accounting increments only after effects, and both supplier receipt and campaign debit are checked for an exact transfer; token failure or a non-exact movement reverts the accounting change. Rejected deployments may retry before D only with a fresh receipt ID; PB-16 continues to reject receipt replay.
- **Timeout risk:** Reviewer silence rejects an otherwise valid claim and releases its reservation. This can disadvantage an honest claimant, as already documented in the campaign trust model; no silence path awards funds.
