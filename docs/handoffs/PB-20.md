## Contracts and wire format

- `contracts/pb20/messaging/CampaignMessageSender.sol` fixes the destination selector, receiver, campaign and policy hash at construction. The policy's `sourceRegistry` must be this sender. Only the immutable reporter can send; quotes and sends use the router's current native fee, and sends include no tokens.
- `contracts/pb20/messaging/CampaignMessageReceiver.sol` inherits Chainlink CCIP v2.0 `CCIPReceiver`, accepts callbacks only from that router, and binds source selector/sender plus campaign and policy fields through the immutable PB-15 registry policy. It can be deployed with the campaign's predicted address before campaign bytecode exists, queue a bounded report, and retry processing after the campaign deploys. A separate permissionless processing call submits to `RemediationCampaign.submitClaim`, preserving PB-16 verification and campaign accounting. Expired/late pending reports can be dropped; terminal messages cannot be revived.
- Wire data is `abi.encode(WireMessageV1)` in this exact order: `uint16 schemaVersion` (1), `uint8 outcome` (1 = accepted), `bytes32 businessMessageId` (`keccak256(abi.encode(campaignId, deploymentKey, receiptId))`), full `PatchBondTypes.AcceptanceReceipt`, EIP-712 signature bytes, and eligibility proof. The business ID is derived again on receipt; CCIP's transport `messageId` is tracked separately.
- The local Foundry remapping uses the exact required Chainlink CCIP `2.0.0` Solidity sources under `contracts/lib/chainlink-ccip/`, retaining upstream license and license-grant notices. The Arc Studio app already has the `2.0.0` package and its `remappings.txt` entry.
